# Hisab Kitab privacy policy

_Last updated: 29 September 2026_

Hisab Kitab ("the app") is an offline expense and budget tracker. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Information the app handles

You may enter the following into the app:

- Transactions: amounts, categories, dates, times and notes
- Categories, budgets and preferences such as your currency, theme and language

This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app has no servers, no user accounts and no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking, and it does not collect or share personal or device information.

## Device backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup, such as your Google account backup. That backup is managed by Android and your backup provider, not by the app.

## App lock

If you turn on app lock, the app asks Android to verify you with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app never sees or stores your biometric data or your PIN, pattern or password.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data leaves the device.

## Exporting your data

When you export to CSV, the file is saved to the location you choose through the system file picker. After that, you decide what happens to the file.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual transactions and categories in the app. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
